# Twelve-channel generation: timeout and optional-evidence repair

The normal shared launcher serves ten independent owner agents plus the Daily and legacy companion slots. Each owner retains the full provider-day calendar, all twelve connected source families, its own candidate selection, and both simulation tools. The old Daily and legacy Windows timers remain disabled.

## Behavior

- Source-backed PLAN calls have a finite 600-second ceiling; FINAL and legacy inline calls retain 300 seconds. Remaining overall time always shortens a call. There are still at most twenty owner requests and no automatic retry.
- Independent data reads may run in parallel through the existing tools. Queries use owner-chosen field projections; every proposed candidate still requires the complete original record. Calendar coverage and data access limits are unchanged.
- Missing optional weather, XI, news, venue detail or simulation support is explicit local uncertainty. An owner ranks its valid research options instead of adding a qualitative veto. Price, identity, kickoff, required model, product, source-reading and receipt checks remain binding. This does not grant READY status or wager authority.
- The normal decision budget is at most 4,800 seconds within a 7,200-second business guard. The worker passes an absolute owner deadline 300 seconds before its actual business deadline, reserving time to publish and save. A human-imposed earlier deadline still wins. The reserve is not a wall-time guarantee.
- Owners that have not started when the deadline expires receive an explicit not-started result. Completed choices are retained; an earlier abstention or failed call is never converted into a selection.
- A large optional dashboard snapshot no longer hides saved canonical cards. Its overview is marked UNKNOWN; corruption and identity mismatches remain errors.
- The new morning task's finite definition allows 135 minutes for the same business worker, verification and shutdown. Changing the source does not register or enable a task. Enablement follows successful real delivery and public readback.

## Validation and remaining acceptance

Twenty new controls passed in an isolated copied-source environment, including the actual builder, publication and JSON/Markdown writer for completed selections with nine expired owners. No real model, provider, production database or task operation was used in those controls. Two previously successful unchanged dashboard controls are reused explicitly. Default lint and PowerShell syntax checks passed through preserved, source-bound results; the original test-style lint failure remains recorded.

These controls establish the code behavior, not successful daily delivery. Deployment, a new genuine normal run, physical provenance verification, and dashboard readback remain separate acceptance steps. Twelve distinct main slots must satisfy minimum product odds 10 and at most six distinct fixtures; 15–20 remains a preference. Profit is unproven and no wager is placed.

The earlier stopped run remains native 124 with no new canonical report. Its journal, abstention, timeout, and all previous reports are preserved. This repair authorizes no replay of that request.
